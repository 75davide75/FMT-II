## Description
FindMyTeacher is your school companion: **timetable, diary and grades** in a single app, fast and completely offline. No account, no server — your data stays on your device.

TIMETABLE
• Import your school timetable PDF: the app turns it into a searchable schedule automatically.
• Search by class or by teacher and instantly see where each teacher is.
• Three views: Now (current and next lesson), Day and Week.
• No PDF? Build your timetable by hand with the visual editor.

DIARY
• Jot down homework, tests and oral exams with a rich text editor.
• Link each entry to subject, teacher, day and time.
• Local‑notification reminders so you never miss a deadline.

GRADES
• Record your grades and track your average, term by term.
• A trend chart for every subject.
• Customizable pass threshold and a colour per subject.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Works completely offline.
• No data collected, no tracking, no ads.
• Back up and export your data to a file whenever you want.

Built for students and teachers who want their whole school life in one place, with a polished, privacy‑respecting interface.
